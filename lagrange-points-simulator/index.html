STANDBY — choose a system, then place a test mass at any Lagrange point (or click the map) to test its stability.
ILLUSTRATIVE MODEL — units are the standard CRTBP normalization (G=1, total mass=1, separation=1, orbital angular velocity=1; one time unit = 1/2π of an orbital period). L1/L2/L3 are solved with a bracketed bisection on the effective-potential equilibrium condition (guaranteed to converge for every μ); L4/L5 are exact equilateral-triangle points. Test-mass motion integrates the full rotating-frame equations, including the Coriolis term, with an RK4 solver whose step size shrinks near the masses. The Jacobi constant C = 2Ω − v² is conserved by the true dynamics, so ΔC measures numerical error. Bodies are not drawn to scale; test masses that come within a small capture radius of a body are stopped. Planar, circular-orbit approximation. Physical distances use mean orbital separations.
