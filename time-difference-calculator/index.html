History

How do I count the days until my trip?

Open the Countdown tab, pick the date and time, add a name such as Trip, and press Save. The days, hours, minutes and seconds update every second, and the countdown stays saved on your device.

How do I find the time between two dates?

Use the Time Difference tab. Enter a start and an end, and you get the difference in days, hours, minutes and seconds, plus the same gap as total weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds.

How do I time how long something lasted?

Use the Start / End tab, for example to see how long your phone lasts after a full charge. Press Start when you begin and End when you finish. The result is kept in your history.

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. It uses your device's time zone, so if the clocks changed between the two moments, the result shows the real time that passed.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your countdowns, dates and history are saved only in your browser on this device. Clearing your browser data removes them. See the Privacy Policy.
