03 // ABOUT

About this explorer

Star System Explorer is a free 3D guide to real planetary systems beyond the Sun. Pick a system such as TRAPPIST-1, Kepler-11, Kepler-90, HR 8799, 55 Cancri, Kepler-16 or PSR B1257+12 to see its planets in orbit, its habitable zone, and how it compares with our own Solar System.

Orbit sizes come from published discovery papers and space-agency releases. Where the exact position of a planet along its orbit isn't known, the starting positions are illustrative. Want to design a system of your own? Open the Star System Creator.

Distances and orbits are rounded from published sources. The habitable zone uses a simple flux model (0.53–1.1× the light Earth receives), the same one as the Star System Creator.
