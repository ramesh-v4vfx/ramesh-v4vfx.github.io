RAMESHVERSE

RameshVerse is a set of free tools. There are no accounts, no logins and no ads. This page explains, in plain words, what happens with data when you use the site.

1. Your tool data stays on your device

Tools such as notes, checklists, routines, logs, timers, and your recent and favorite tools save their data in your own browser (local storage). It is not sent to our servers, and we cannot see it, with one exception: reminders (see section 3). Clearing your browser data deletes it, and it does not move to other devices or browsers on its own.

2. Anonymous visit statistics

We use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not follow you across other sites. It records technical details such as the page visited, where you came from, your country, browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language. It also records a few interactions, such as using the home-page banner. We use this only to see how the site is used, for example which tools are popular. We do not sell this data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Other services your browser contacts

  • Google Fonts: the site loads its font from Google, so Google receives your IP address and browser details to deliver it.
  • Cloudflare Workers: the home page asks a small service we run on Cloudflare for the list of popular tools.
  • Reminders (only if you allow notifications): if you turn on notifications (for Today's Notes, Calendar, or the timer tools), the reminder text (for Today's Notes the text of your open notes, for Calendar your event titles, notes and reminder times), your browser's push address and a random ID (created by this site and kept in your browser) are sent to a small notification service we run on Cloudflare, so reminders can arrive when the page is closed. They are delivered through your browser's own push service (run by Google, Apple or Mozilla, depending on your browser). If notifications are not allowed, nothing is sent. Tools with reminders use the same service.
  • Read aloud (My Diary): the text is read by the voices built into your browser or device. Voices marked "(online)" in the list may send the text to the browser maker's servers to turn it into speech. The other voices run on your device, and the diary picks one of those by default.
  • Live-data tools: a few tools may request public data (for example currency rates or sky data) from the provider of that data.
  • YouTube and social links: if you click a link to YouTube or a share button, you are then on that service and its own policy applies.

4. Cookies

The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Local storage is used only to keep your own tool data and settings on your device. The site also registers a service worker, a small helper script your browser keeps for reminders and offline use. It stores copies of pages from this site that you visit, on your device only, so they can open without a connection.

5. Feedback and contact

There is no feedback form on this site and we do not collect messages here. To contact us, use the YouTube channel. Anything you post there, such as a comment, is public and is handled by YouTube under its own policy.

6. Your choices

You can clear the data of this site at any time in your browser settings, and switch off notifications for the site there to stop reminders. You can also use a content blocker if you prefer not to be counted in the statistics.

7. Changes

If this changes, for example if accounts, sync or donations are added, this page will be updated first.
