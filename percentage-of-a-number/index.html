What this does: it scales a number by a percentage — telling you how much X% actually comes out to when applied to a value.
Example: a shop offers 15% off a ₹2,400 jacket. Percentage = 15, Value = 2400 → discount is ₹360, so you pay ₹2,040 (the "Value − the amount" tile). Same idea works for tips, tax, interest, or commission on any amount; "Value + the amount" gives the total after adding tax or a tip.
