ILLUSTRATIVE MODEL — semi-major axis from Kepler's third law, the light curve from a limb-darkened stellar disc (quadratic law) integrated numerically under the planet's disc, duration from the transit chord, habitable-zone bounds from stellar luminosity scaling, equilibrium temperature with albedo 0.3. Photometric noise is simulated. Between transits the animation runs fast, and it slows down during each one. Planets and orbits are drawn larger than true scale for visibility (switch to true scale to see how tiny an Earth is). Not a substitute for pipelines like Kepler/TESS SPOC.
