The sun's position (declination, equation of time and distance) comes from a low-precision astronomical algorithm; the globe's spin is tied directly to your system clock, not a looped animation. The Moon's position and phase come from a low-precision lunar algorithm (about 0.3° accuracy); Earth, Moon and Sun are drawn at true size and true distance, so the Moon and Sun are small from afar and Earth is only a dot from the Sun's surface (a small marker shows where). The Moon's near side always faces Earth (no libration). The ocean glint, twilight band and atmospheric glow are approximations for visual realism.
