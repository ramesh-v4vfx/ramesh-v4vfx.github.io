Mealtime & correction dose

Enter your carb ratio, correction factor and target glucose (as prescribed by your care team) in Settings to use the calculator.

How it's calculated

Meal dose = carbs ÷ carb ratio. Correction = (glucose − target) ÷ correction factor. Insulin on board (rapid doses you logged, decaying linearly over your insulin action time) is subtracted. The result is rounded to your dose step. It is an estimate from what you've entered — always compare with your own plan.
